## Project Showcase

### U.S. State Income vs. Poverty (2022–2023)
- **Research question**: How large are the gaps in median household income and poverty rates across U.S. states, and has the gap between the highest- and lowest-income states widened between 2022 and 2023?
- **Data**: USDA ERS state-level data on median household income and poverty rates (2022–2023).
- **Visualizations**:
  1. Ranked bar chart of 2023 median household income by state.
  2. Income vs. poverty rate scatter plot.
  3. Top-5 vs. bottom-5 states slope chart from 2022 to 2023.
- **Findings**: The income gap between the top-5 and bottom-5 states widened from 2022 to 2023. States with higher median income tend to have lower poverty rates.
